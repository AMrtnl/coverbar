CoverBar 2.0

A rebuilt player for macOS 26 Tahoe.

  • Liquid Glass player in three layouts: vertical, horizontal and mini. Switch from the player itself; the panel reshapes in place, its top edge locked under the menu bar.
  • Synced lyrics that follow the song. Click a line to jump there.
  • Up Next: the following songs and their covers, beside the vertical player or under the other two. Click one to play it, or press U to show the list.
  • Sound in the player, laid out like Control Center: the volume, your Mac's speakers and AirPods, and with Music, every AirPlay speaker and TV, each with its own icon.
  • Tint the glass light, dark or with the cover's own color, or turn the whole panel into the artwork.
  • Quick Settings in the player, and a new Settings window with a live preview.
