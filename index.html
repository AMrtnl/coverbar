Questions

Which Macs does it run on?
Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 26 Tahoe or later. The player is built on macOS 26's Liquid Glass, so earlier versions aren't supported.
Which music apps work?
Anything that shows in Control Center's Now Playing: Apple Music, Spotify, Podcasts, and audio or video playing in Safari, Chrome and other browsers. Up Next needs a player that shares its queue with macOS, as Apple Music does; with others the list stays empty.
How does the free trial work?
Everything is unlocked for 7 days, with no account and no card. Quick Settings and Settings → License show exactly how much time is left.
What happens when the trial ends?
The cover in the menu bar keeps working. The player and lyrics ask for a license, which you can enter in Settings → License.
How do I move my license to a new Mac?
On the old Mac, open Settings → License and choose Deactivate This Mac. Then enter the same key on the new one. Your key works on two Macs at a time.
Does CoverBar send my listening data anywhere?
No. There's no account and no analytics. When lyrics are on screen, CoverBar asks LRCLIB, a free lyrics database, for that song's lyrics and keeps what it finds on your Mac.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. If CoverBar isn't for you, write to support@coverbar.appwithin 14 days of buying and we'll refund you.
