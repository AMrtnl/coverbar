Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

CoverBar is a menu bar app for macOS. It is built to keep what you listen to on your Mac. This policy explains the little data it handles and why.

What stays on your Mac

CoverBar reads what's playing from macOS (the same information Control Center shows): song title, artist, album, artwork, playback position, and which app is playing. It uses this only to draw the cover in your menu bar and the player. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your settings, and any lyrics CoverBar has already looked up, are stored on your Mac.

Lyrics

When lyrics are on screen and CoverBar doesn't have them yet, it asks LRCLIB, a free, community-run lyrics database, for that song. The request contains the song's title, artist, album and length, and, like any web request, your IP address. LRCLIB doesn't receive an account or any identifier from CoverBar. If you never show lyrics, CoverBar never contacts LRCLIB.

AirPlay speakers

When you open Sound in the player while Music is playing, CoverBar asks Music (with your permission) which AirPlay speakers and TVs it can play to, and plays to the one you pick. To show each with the right icon, it reads the model that AirPlay devices announce on your local network. This stays on your network: nothing about your devices is sent to us or anyone else.

Matching the glass to your desktop

If you turn on the setting that adapts the glass to what's behind the player, macOS asks you for screen recording permission. CoverBar uses it only to measure how light or dark the area behind the player is, on your Mac, at the moment the player opens. It never records, saves or sends any image.

Purchases and license keys

CoverBar is sold through Polar, which acts as our reseller and handles payment, receipts and taxes. When you buy, Polar processes your name, email address, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details so we can help you with your purchase.

To activate a license, CoverBar sends your license key and your Mac's name (as set in System Settings → General → About) to Polar, so you can tell your two activations apart. It checks the key again every few days, and keeps working offline for up to 30 days.

No analytics

CoverBar has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. This website doesn't use tracking cookies either.
